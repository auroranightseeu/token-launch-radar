G = exp(g/100) if negative; otherwise 1 + 0.35 × ln(1 + g/100)
M = 0.65 + 0.55 × (1 − exp(−age/540))

Scale has diminishing returns. Outflows hurt more than inflows help. Maturity saturates at 1.20×.
